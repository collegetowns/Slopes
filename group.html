Your trail map

  1. Build your group. Choose how many people are playing, then everyone picks a character.
  2. Draw a case. Policies come up at random. Peek at the next three on the right, ride the one you want, or skip one. Keep going as long as your group likes.
  3. Take turns. Each person clicks where the case fits: left = precise, right = flexible; top = federal, middle = state, bottom = local. Others' picks stay hidden until everyone has locked in.
  4. See where you land. The app averages your spots and places the case on the spectrum. Flip to Individual Run any time to see each person's pick.
  5. Hit the Lodge when you're done, then print your lift pass or send it to your teacher in Microsoft Teams.
